## Changes from v2.2.0

- Reconciling the Schwarzschild Solution with Conservation of Mass-Energy, version 1.0.1: the law of free motion is written as the one law on both sides of L, \((dr/d\tau)^2=c^2(e^2-|1-L/r|)\), in the two places that still showed the outside form \(c^2(e^2-1+L/r)\): Section 6, statement 3 ("One metric for every observer"), where Section 14, Part 7 names that form general relativity's law for the inside, and Section 14, Part 5, the far time of the passage, as in that section's box for a theoretical physicist. Outside L the two forms are the same, so every figure stands. The page, its folder copy and its text copy change; nothing else in the paper does.
- New in Relativity: Curved Space from f · dr.
- The six articles of v2.2.0: their list of the series now names Curved Space from f · dr; their text is unchanged.
- New in Internal Spaces: Color Charge from a Wave on an Oriented Internal Loop.
- Every other file is unchanged. The README, in three forms, and the landing page are not frozen, because they carry this release's DOI, which Zenodo mints after the release.
