## Changes from v1.1.0

- A new title: "Why Is It Impossible to Escape from a Black Hole?". The subtitle and the note under the title are unchanged.
- The Introduction ("Introduction and Roadmap of the Paper") is three explanations, for a child, a high school physics teacher and a theoretical physicist, of counting steps against the size of the ruler, in place of its opening paragraphs and earlier explanations.
- The roadmap: Part I (Introduction) — Why the No-Escape Proof Needs the Same Yardstick and Number of Steps; Part I — Step-Count Equivalence Across Frames; Part II — Yardstick and Scale Non-Equivalence Across Frames, now with the worked trajectory examples; Part III — Relativistic Reconciliation, Causal Boundaries, and Intermediate Frames.
- Chapter 1 is "Part I — Step-Count Equivalence Across Frames": the derivation of L = 2Gℳ/c², explained at the same three levels. Chapter 2 adds that everyone agrees on the number of steps to get out.
- The former Chapters 3 ("Transition: The Shared Yardstick and Hidden Assumptions") and 4 ("Why the Horizon Need Not Be Shared Without a Common Yardstick") are removed.
- "Setup for the Thought Experiment" (Chapter 5) holds the former Chapters 7 and 8 as its subsections.
- Chapter 8 (former 11) adds three explanations of why ruling out redshift inside the horizon is circular. Chapter 9 (former 12) is three explanations of why observers A and B have different size rulers, pointing to Appendix A, A.1, which derives the rule for frames held at rest inside the event horizon.
- The chapters are renumbered 1 to 24; every chapter reference, Appendix A, the AI-review sections and the brief for AI reviewers follow the new numbers.
- A display fix: lists inside the roadmap's cards no longer take the cards' numbering.
