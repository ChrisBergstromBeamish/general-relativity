## Changes from v1.0.0

- A note under the title: the proof that general relativity uses to assert that nothing can escape a black hole requires all frames of reference inside the black hole to share the same yardstick.
- Before the Introduction: a brief for AI reviewers, and three sections: why an AI reviewer can't blindly review this paper, why AI can't write this paper, and how the AI review worked.
- The Introduction opens with the hidden yardstick assumption, explained for a child, a high school physics teacher and a theoretical physicist; the roadmap has four parts, and the new Part I carries the same three explanations. The Introduction's original paragraph on the hidden assumptions now ends Chapter 3.
- Corrections and completions from the AI review in Chapters 18, 19, 21, 22, 24, 25, 26 and 27 and in the second reviewer appendix, among them a ratio written upside down in Chapter 18, values in Chapter 22 that did not come from the chapter's own formula, and a false statement in Chapter 25 about where a continuous function is zero.
- Appendix A (new): the complete update rule and the A → B → C → D calculation, with the derivation of the scale law for frames held at rest inside the event horizon.
- The page's "Copy text" source carries the same changes.
