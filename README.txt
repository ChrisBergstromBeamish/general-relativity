VALIDATION

* NASA NICER data
  https://github.com/ChrisBergstromBeamish/fast-nicer-pulse-profile-modeling
  The pulse-profile calculation that the paper tests on NASA's NICER observations of neutron stars (the paper's Sections 15 and 16): the programs, the test stars and the measured timings.
  Last uploaded 2026-11-23 20:12:41 UTC.

* Redshift data for observations with large redshifts
  https://github.com/ChrisBergstromBeamish/general-relativity/blob/main/validation/high-redshift-observations.md
  The observations with the highest redshifts of light from the surfaces of compact bodies and from the disks around them, each with its source and status, as in the paper's Section 19; the comparison is in its Section 17.
  Uploaded 2026-11-26 23:49:16 UTC.
