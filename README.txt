DECAY

* Decay by Collapse on Separate Internal Spaces
  https://chrisbergstrombeamish.github.io/general-relativity/Decay/
  What decay is: collapse, again and again, until one pattern is two. A particle decays when one of its repeated collapses lands its internal spaces far enough apart to be two complete particles. The same chance at every collapse gives the exponential law; a particle whose short list of outcomes from Granularity holds no two-particle outcome never decays; counting on the particle's own clock gives the longer life of a moving one; and each landing is weighted by the square of the wave function on curved space. No new internal space is needed. The measured lifetimes are anchors: each fixes the rate of its particle. The document lists what the constructions still have to give. First published in release v2.5.0 of this folder, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24602940.
