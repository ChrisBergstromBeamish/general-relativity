MS-A1 · Measurements. A twist, plate displacement, count, phase difference, wavelength or frequency ratio is the starting datum in §1. MS-A3 · Clocks and complete moving bodies. Let \(d\tau_h=f\,dt\), \(d\ell^2=h_{ij}dX^i dX^j\), and \(v_{\rm loc}=d\ell/d\tau_h\). Local SR gives

\[ d\tau=d\tau_h\sqrt{1-v_{\rm loc}^2/c^2},\qquad q=f/\gamma=\sqrt{f^2-h_{ij}\dot X^i\dot X^j/c^2}. \]

A timelike centre has \(q>0\). On an ideal ray \(d\ell=c\,d\tau_h\), hence \(d\tau=0\). Gravity-paper premise 2 also gives \(H_{\rm loc}=\gamma E_0\) for the complete body. It applies to both endpoint states of a loading experiment. This supplies MS-O2–O5.

MS-A4 · General centre coupling. Let \(y\) denote all the store coordinates and integrate over the internal measure where appropriate. At fixed \(y,\dot y\),

\[ L_t=qL_0(y,\dot y/q),\qquad y'=\dot y/q,\qquad \partial_q L_t=L_0-\sum_a y'_a\frac{\partial L_0}{\partial y'_a}=-H_0 . \]

\[ \frac{dH_0}{d\tau} =\sum_a y'_a\left[\frac{d}{d\tau} \frac{\partial L_0}{\partial y'_a}-\frac{\partial L_0}{\partial y_a}\right]=0. \]

Thus \(H_0=E_0\) can remain constant while \(L_0\) oscillates. This supplies MS-O2 and the sum in MS-O3.

MS-A5 · Spin. Restrict a homogeneous harmonic polynomial \(r^\ell Y\) in four dimensions to the unit three-sphere. The radial and angular parts of its Laplacian give

\[ 0=r^{\ell-2}\big[\ell(\ell+2)Y+\Delta_{S^3}Y\big]. \]

A monomial of bi-degree \((p,r')\) has \(\ell=p+r'\) and right character \(k=r'-p\), so \(\ell=|k|+2n\). Removing radial multiples leaves \((p+1)(r'+1)-pr'=\ell+1\) harmonics at fixed character. The left rotation representation has \(j=\ell/2\). The wave action gives

\[ \omega_\ell=\Omega\sqrt{\ell(\ell+2)},\qquad J=I\omega_\ell A^2,\qquad E=J\omega_\ell . \]

The elementary harmonic has fixed \(J=s_0>0\). Its physical calibration is \(s_0=\hbar\). For \(j=0\), \(\ell=0\), the profile is constant and \(\omega_\ell=0\). It carries no positive-action oscillatory store. This is the spin-zero step in MS-O2 and MS-O4. Spin from a Specified Internal Geometry, §§12–13
