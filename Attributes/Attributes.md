A reading of the wave is the attribute measured. ### How each verdict is reached

**The test for a new space.** For a measured reading \(Y\) and the readings \(R\) that the existing spaces export, a function \(b\) with \(Y=b(R)\) exists on the realized preparations exactly when
\[
R(h)=R(h')\ \Longrightarrow\ Y(h)=Y(h') .
\]
Necessity follows by applying \(b\). For sufficiency, give each realized reading the \(Y\) of any of its preparations; the implication makes the choice of preparation irrelevant. **DERIVED.** So the test works in two directions. Where a document exhibits the existing reading that carries an attribute, a new space is unnecessary for it. A new space would be shown to be needed by two independently prepared states that agree on every existing state, comparison and process history and still differ in a measured reading that predicts another measurement. No row below has such a pair. That direction cannot yet be used in full: no whole particle has yet been given its state on all seven spaces, so for now the test can show that a new space is unnecessary for a reading the spaces exhibit, and cannot yet show that one is needed.

**Labels.** Each result carries the label its document gives it. **DERIVED**: it follows by the mathematics shown, from the premises named. **OBSERVED**: an instrument reading. **IMPORT**: taken from outside the theory, such as an experimental reduction, a standard result or local special relativity. **UNTRACED**: a physical premise whose origin in Geometry, Granularity or Collapse is not yet traced. **HINT**: the author's words. The standard model and quantum field theory enter only as comparisons, never as a judge.

**Two kinds of observation.** The author, 27 November 2026: "We can fix as long as there are parameters to fix. We can never have a rule. A rule means we don't understand something." And: "I didn't make the rule. I never make rules. The observations above can be one of two things. 1. Constraints used to help us choose geomery, or 2. observations that can be used as anchors to fully numerically define the geometry." Every observation below is one of the two. An observation is an anchor only where a construction has the constant it would fix. Where a derivation stops, the subsection says what is not yet understood and which construction has to give it.

## 2. The answer

**The seven internal spaces stand so far.** For every one of the 31 attributes on the list, no new internal space is shown to be needed, and for most of them the documents show that none is needed: they exhibit the existing reading that carries the attribute. Several results are exact consequences of the spaces as built. On one spin doublet a magnetic moment can only point along the spin. A full turn of a charge wave in its value plane has action exactly \(2\pi J\). A store on its own clock gives inertia \(E_0/c^2\) and falls with the same coefficient. Light's two polarizations need no internal space, because spacetime has two directions across every ray, while every spin-carrying sector of the spin space has rest energy. Decay is the first collapse that leaves one parent as two or more complete particles. Other attributes are, so far, observations that constrain an existing space, such as the electron's 1.00116 turns of spin for each turn of its velocity, or that fix a preparation, such as the 125 GeV of the Higgs; either becomes an anchor of a constant of a space only once a construction contains that constant. What is not yet understood is construction work inside the seven spaces, on spacetime for light, and in the joint wave of two patterns. The table names it space by space.

### What this touches in general relativity

- **Equal fall.** General relativity takes the equal response of every body to gravity as a premise: universal coupling to one metric. Here it is derived. A store whose action runs on its centre's own clock has the same coefficient \(E_0/c^2\) in its inertia and in the pull on it, so \(E_0\) cancels from its fall ([Spin, §17](../Internal-Spaces/Spin-from-Internal-Geometry/#gravity); [What “mass” is made of, §14](What-Mass-Is-Made-Of/#verdict)). The observation that could decide between the premise and the derivation is a composition-dependent release signal after an independently measured change of storage. The torsion-balance and MICROSCOPE tests found none at their precision. Equal fall by itself is no proof of general relativity's premise.
- **Light.** General relativity propagates light along null geodesics of one spacetime geometry. Here the redshift and the angular law of light follow from the gravity paper's clocks, rulers and crests ([Photon, §14](The-Photon/#e6); [What “mass” is made of, §14](What-Mass-Is-Made-Of/#verdict)).
- **The pull a body makes.** General relativity's stress-energy source is a separate premise. Here the pull counts each energy once, in one observer's units. A closed, static box of light cannot decide between the two. An observation of the pull of a region with unbalanced stress, with its supports and tractions accounted for independently, could ([What “mass” is made of, §14](What-Mass-Is-Made-Of/#verdict)).
- **One horizon.** No observation in this series supplies the event that would test the step by which general relativity merges the frames' different boundary readings into one event horizon: an outgoing signal whose emission was independently located inside the proposed common boundary. On these observations that step is unproven ([What “mass” is made of, §14](What-Mass-Is-Made-Of/#verdict); [Exclusion, §1](Exclusion/)).
- **Stars.** The gravitational parameter read from a Shapiro delay and the radius read from a NICER pulse profile carry general relativity's exterior inside them. They are **IMPORT**, never anchors (§3.11).
- **Decay near a body.** General relativity takes as a premise that a local process runs alike on its own clock wherever it is held, so that the clock factor is the whole effect of height; it is part of the equivalence principle. Here a held parent's clock changes how many collapses fall in a second, and the share at each collapse is a separate factor: more physical volume on the separating outcomes, relative to the surviving ones, raises the chance of decay at that collapse. The sign and the size of that change near a body are not yet understood: the gravity paper has not yet joined the volume a body curves to the volume a pattern's own wave curves. The observation that could decide is matched parents held at rest at two heights, compared after the measured clock factor is removed ([Decay, §12](../Decay/#lt-clock)).
- **The geometry of a spreading wave.** The chance of landing in a region is its share of physical volume (§3.22). For the specified initial data of the gravity paper, general relativity's constraint equation (**IMPORT**, comparison only) requires one constant spatial curvature, while the probability geometry of a spreading Gaussian wave has a curvature that changes sign at \(\lvert x\rvert=3s\): the two disagree. The gravity paper gives a different, non-Gaussian geometry that satisfies the comparison. The premise touched is that the geometry of space must satisfy general relativity's constraint equations. No measurement of the proper volumes of a spreading wave has tested it; independent measurements of volumes and directional lengths could decide ([Decay, §14](../Decay/#s-14)).
- **Changes at once.** The published Collapse completion C-TIME (**UNTRACED**) updates both supports of a two-daughter event at one common time, against the premise that a change must move at the speed of light. The paired counts of §3.31, with the analyser settings switched while the pair is in flight, are the observations that bear on the at-once selection of a joint outcome; for clocks and rulers nothing in this series shows that premise false or true.
- **Spin turning.** The local Lorentz comparison of clocks and rulers, a premise general relativity carries, admits different transports of the spin on the same centre trajectory, so it cannot entail that a magnetic setting turns spin and velocity equally. \mathbf P=\frac{\gamma E_I\mathbf V}{C^2},\qquad
\left.\frac{\partial P_i}{\partial V_j}\right|_{\mathbf V=0}=\frac{E_I}{C^2}\,\delta_{ij}.

### 3.3 Electric charge, its sign and the electric response

\[
\oint_{\rm turn}\Theta=\int_0^{2\pi}J\,d\psi=2\pi J .

\[
e^{2\pi iJ/s_*}=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad J=ms_*,\quad m\in\mathbb Z .

## 4. The documents of the series

1. [Decay by collapse on separate internal spaces](../Decay/). A particle decays when one of its repeated collapses lands its internal spaces far enough apart to be two complete particles: the same chance at every collapse gives the exponential law, a short list with no two-particle outcome on it gives a particle that never decays, and counting on the particle's own clock gives the longer life of a moving one.

3. [Exclusion and the complete state of two electrons](Exclusion/).
