Abstract. Rotate a charge wave and its momentum together through one turn in the wave’s oriented plane. If the action phase returns around this turn for every complete pattern, then \(J=m\hbar\), with \(m\in\mathbb Z\). One electric calibration then gives one step for every family, whatever the interval length, wave speed, action coefficient, mode or polarization. 8. Summary for three readers

For a child. The wave can turn either way, or swing back and forth. That gives positive charge, negative charge, or a moving wave with zero charge. Turn the whole wave around once and its turning action tells you how much charge it carries. If its phase comes back in step after that turn, whole particles can carry only whole amounts.
