**Abstract.** Photon polarization needs no additional internal space. For each direction of travel, the geometry of \(M^{3,1}\) supplies two independent directions across the ray. A vector in that plane returns to itself after one full turn. There is a separate physical question about why light occupies precisely that plane. It supplies the physical two-state restriction, the energy \(E=\omega Q\), the momentum \(\mathbf P=\mathbf KQ\), and the circular angular momentum \(S_\parallel=\pm Q\). The import supplies one such law, because the travelling pattern's own law is not built. The readings \(h\nu\), \(h\nu/c\), and \(\pm\hbar\) further require one whole unit of travelling action with the calibration used on the spin space.

The spin construction gives an exact exclusion. Every occupied sector that carries spin has positive rest energy, including on the published travelling channel. ## 18. What is not yet understood

- **The travelling value and its dynamics.** Geometry on \(M^{3,1}\) must produce its physical components, speed, energy, momentum and rotation reading.
- **The absence of a third state.** Geometry must exclude it as a wave, or Granularity must exclude its occupation by a complete photon. Collapse and the analyser must explain the observed support if the wave remains physically possible.
- **The value for an observer in motion.** Geometry on \(M^{3,1}\) must give the comparison of polarization values between observers.
- **One whole action unit.** Granularity of the travelling pattern must give its complete actions and the relation of its unit to the spin calibration.
- **A complete optical count.** Collapse of the travelling pattern must give the supported absorber classes, trigger and capacity in an optical apparatus.
- **Emission, capture and coherent sign.** The overlap of the charge pattern, the charge receiver and the pattern on \(M^{3,1}\) must give the outgoing coefficients, supported transitions and the sign relative to a charged reference.
- **Two alike patterns.** Geometry of a physical joint wave must supply its domain and the exchange character carried from a direction-free preparation to the splitter.
