# Handedness in the weak interaction

*Attributes · 27 November 2026*

**Abstract.** Handedness needs no internal space of its own. For a spin-half packet with nonzero momentum, the existing spin wave supplies both projections along the direction of motion, in the same winding. Its two components accumulate the same proper-time phase. For a packet with positive rest energy, the rotation lift and local special relativity also determine how two observers compare its spin on specified rest axes. There are exactly two smooth, complex-linear boost representations on this doublet that depend only on the change of observer. On an unpolarized input their normalized intensities are \((1+s\lambda v/c)/2\), where \(\lambda=\pm1\) is the projection and \(s=\pm1\) distinguishes the representations. The negative sign gives the full electron reference curve with one sign choice. What makes a particular weak transition read that combination remains to be derived. Cobalt-60 polarimetry gives a share of \(0.158\pm0.020\) along the electron’s motion at 194 keV. Radium E gives a different polarization coefficient. The distinction between a spin reading, a response assigned to it, and a response predicted by a construction determines the verdict.

## 2. Method

1. **Carry one packet, then change observer.** Hold the spin action and rest-axis preparation fixed. A laboratory clock measures \(t\), and the two internal oscillator components experience \(\tau=\int q_t\,dt\). Compare their evolved amplitudes. At one event, compare two observers’ rotation-free boosts from rest and calculate the rotation left between their rest axes. These operations answer different parts of [WH-O1](#o1).
2. **Mirror the apparatus and preparation together.** At equal laboratory time reverse spatial position and momentum. Carry each analyser mark with its apparatus. Then, in a separate preparation, reverse only an internal plane, loop or spin reference. Calculate which exported readings change sign in each operation. [WH-O2](#o2)
3. **Compare counts under the reversal actually made.** Resolve both spin projections at fixed energy bin and acceptance. Reverse the parent for Wu, the analyser for beta polarimetry and the beam setting for SLD. Retain Goldhaber’s photon analyser and recoil selection. Fix only the combination each apparatus resolves. [WH-O3](#o3) · [WH-O4](#o4) · [WH-O5](#o5)
4. **Compare two tags after free flight.** Prepare specified quadratures of flavour modes. Propagate them to a detector at a measured baseline and project the wave onto the detector tag. Repeat with orthogonal pure-mode tags. The comparison determines when propagation can produce a changing count. [WH-O6](#o6)

## 9. What a boost does to the spin packet

### The clock advances both components by one phase

Write the stationary spin coefficients as \(A e^{-i\theta}u\), with \(u^\dagger u=1\) fixed during free translation. Eliminating the cyclic phase at fixed action gives

\[
Q=\frac{IA^2\dot\theta}{q_t},\qquad

\]

Stationarity in \(A>0\) gives \(A^2=Q/(I\omega_0)\). Each term in parentheses is then \(\omega_0Q/2\). Hence

\[
E_0=\omega_0Q,\qquad
\mathcal R_{\rm centre}=-E_0q_t,\qquad
\dot\theta=\omega_0q_t,\qquad
u_{\rm wave}(t)=e^{-i\omega_0\int q_t\,dt}u_{\rm wave}(0).
\]

Differentiating the reduced action gives \(\mathbf p=E_0\mathbf v/(c^2q_t)\) and \(H=E_0/q_t\). The independent inertia calculation in A7 has the same form with its own rest energy \(E_*\).

\[
(H,cp_\parallel)=E_{\rm rest}(\cosh\eta,\sinh\eta).
\]

An observer moving with rapidity \(\xi\) along that axis measures rapidity \(\eta-\xi\). The centre equations supply the momentum in both frames. To compare spin, one must also compare their axes.

### Exactly two observer-only boost actions on one doublet

**DERIVED on P4 and P6**, in the following class.    [J_i,K_j]=i\epsilon_{ijk}K_k,\qquad

### The normalized packet transforms by a rotation

For \(E_{\rm rest}>0\), form the dimensionless momentum matrix and its positive square root,

\[
P(p)=\frac{H1+c\mathbf p\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma}{E_{\rm rest}},
\qquad
\det P=1,\qquad
L(p)=P(p)^{1/2}=\exp(\eta N/2).
\]

Here \(\eta\ge0\) points along \(\hat{\mathbf p}\). \(L(p)\) is the rotation-free boost from rest. For a frame change \(A\), \(P(p')=AP(p)A^\dagger\). The two routes from rest to \(p'\) differ by

\[
W(A,p)=L(p')^{-1}AL(p),\qquad
WW^\dagger=L(p')^{-1}P(p')L(p')^{-1}=1,\qquad \det W=1.
\]

**It follows that \(W\in SU(2)\) and \(u'=Wu\).** The last step uses the physical rotation lift M1 on the packet’s rest axes. It preserves \(u^\dagger u\) and the fixed action \(Q\). Inserting \(L(p')L(p')^{-1}\) between two frame changes gives
\[
W(A_2A_1,p)=W(A_2,p')W(A_1,p).
\]
If the chosen rest axes are reoriented by \(R(p)\), the component matrix becomes \(R(p')^{-1}W R(p)\). The specified axes therefore matter. This is an observer comparison at one event. Transport along an accelerated trajectory also requires the trajectory’s axes.

For two collinear rotation-free boosts, \(W=1\). The spin on those axes keeps its direction. An observer who overtakes the packet reverses \(\hat{\mathbf p}\), hence reverses \(h=u^\dagger Nu\). At the intervening rest frame \(\hat{\mathbf p}\) is undefined.

For perpendicular boosts of rapidities \(\eta,\xi\), multiplication of the two Pauli exponentials gives a rotation about their normal. The identity and imaginary normal components of the product have ratio \(\tanh(\eta/2)\tanh(\xi/2)=\tan(|\Omega|/2)\). Equivalently
\[
\cos\Omega=\frac{\cosh\eta+\cosh\xi}{1+\cosh\eta\cosh\xi}.
\]
At \(0.9\) and \(1.2\) the angle is \(0.44564\) radians.

A transverse change can also change the magnitude of the longitudinal expectation. Start with \(h=-1\), packet rapidity \(\eta\), and boost the observer transversely by \(\xi\). The polarization four-vector is represented by \(L(p)(\mathbf r\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)L(p)^\dagger\), with \(\mathbf r=u^\dagger\boldsymbol\sigma u\). h'=-\frac{\sinh\eta\cosh\xi}{\sqrt{\cosh^2\eta\cosh^2\xi-1}}.
\]
For \(\eta=0.5\) and \(\xi=0.4,1.2,3.0\), this gives \(-0.808,-0.530,-0.464\).

### A cross-channel test from pion decay

Take a spin-zero positive pion at rest, with two-body final kinematics, a neutrino of negligible rest energy and a definite negative helicity. For back-to-back momenta the common-axis spin projections must sum to zero. The positive charged lepton therefore has negative helicity, its disfavoured projection in the positive-sign response. These are physical conditions on the source and daughters. They are not consequences of the free doublet.

Set \(c=1\) and express rest energies as \(M,m_\ell\). Two-body energy and momentum balance gives \(M=E_\ell+p_\ell\), \(E_\ell^2-p_\ell^2=m_\ell^2\). Consequently
\[
p_\ell=\frac{M^2-m_\ell^2}{2M},\quad
E_\ell=\frac{M^2+m_\ell^2}{2M},\quad
E_\ell-p_\ell=\frac{m_\ell^2}{M}.
\]
The normalized helicity **share** is insufficient for an absolute rate ratio. The additional P14(c) condition is that the covariantly normalized charged intensity carries \(m_\ell e^{-\eta}=E_\ell-p_\ell\), the null daughter intensity carries \(E_\nu=p_\ell\), and the remaining squared source strength is the same for \(e\) and \(\mu\). For the imported two-body measure, integrating the spatial momentum delta function leaves a radial factor proportional to
\[
\frac{p^2}{E_\ell p}\left|\frac{d(E_\ell+p)}{dp}\right|^{-1}
=\frac{p}{M}.
\]

\boxed{\frac{\Gamma_e}{\Gamma_\mu}

## 14. CP violation, flavour mixing and neutrino oscillations

**CP violation.** For real \(w\), \(F_{mn}[w]=\int\phi_m w\phi_n\,d\xi/L\) is real and symmetric. The pure-mode index is unsigned. This isolated export supplies neither a conjugate tag for a neutral pattern nor a phase that changes sign under conjugation.

**Flavour mixing.** Define \(b_n=q_n+i\dot q_n/\omega_n\) from the two quadratures of a real mode. Its free equation gives \(\dot b_n=-i\omega_n b_n\), hence \(b_n(\tau)=b_n(0)e^{-i\omega_n\tau}\). A driven source can separately cause conversion by §11.

**Neutrino oscillations.** Put \(k_n=d_{\beta n}^*s_{\alpha n}\). Direct expansion gives
\[
I_{\beta\alpha}(\tau)=\left|\sum_n k_ne^{-i\omega_n\tau}\right|^2
=\sum_n|k_n|^2+
2\sum_{m<n}\operatorname{Re}\!\left[k_mk_n^*e^{-i(\omega_m-\omega_n)\tau}\right].
\]
For source \((\cos\vartheta,\sin\vartheta)\) and detector \((-\sin\vartheta,\cos\vartheta)\), the amplitude is \(\sin\vartheta\cos\vartheta(e^{-i\omega_2\tau}-e^{-i\omega_1\tau})\), so
\[
I_{\beta\alpha}=\sin^2(2\vartheta)\sin^2[(\omega_2-\omega_1)\tau/2].
\]
Once coherent tags and frequencies are fixed, the oscillation follows. On the constructed interval \(\omega_m-\omega_n=(m-n)\pi v_F/L\). With fixed indices, all frequency gaps share one scale.

This retains the exact \(1/v\). For fixed \(E_*\) and \(v\simeq c\), the leading dependence is \(\ell/H\).
