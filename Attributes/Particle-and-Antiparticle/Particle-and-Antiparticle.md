# What tells a particle from its antiparticle? Baryon number, isospin and signed flavour

**Abstract.** A neutral standing wave on the electric-charge interval can differ from its reflected partner. Individual modes circulate in opposite senses while their total charge vanishes. Five modal circulations can represent \(I_3,B,S,C,B'\) for all 41 classified hadrons. One reflection reverses all five and preserves the wave energy. **The existing charge space has room for these signs.** The five values of each hadron are put in by hand and read back. Of 205 signed entries, zero are obtained independently. Whether hadrons use this room is **not yet understood**. The charge Geometry supplies the possible waves. Its Granularity would have to determine which whole-particle patterns can occur, and the physical preparation or Collapse on that space would have to produce the wave of an independently tagged hadron. The built electric receiver measures only the total. A mode-by-mode proton measurement could distinguish the two halves of equation (1) from the thirds prepared in the colour construction. With the modal tags of (1), free propagation and the specified receiver coupling both give **exactly zero neutral mixing**.

## 8. The same result for three readers
