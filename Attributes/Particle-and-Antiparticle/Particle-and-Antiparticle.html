The built electric receiver measures only the total. A mode-by-mode proton measurement could distinguish the two halves of equation (1) from the thirds prepared in the colour construction. With the modal tags of (1), free propagation and the specified receiver coupling both give exactly zero neutral mixing.

\[ J_n=\mu\epsilon(X_n,\dot X_n),\qquad E_n=\frac{\mu}{2}\bigl(\lvert\dot X_n\rvert^2+\omega_n^2\lvert X_n\rvert^2\bigr),\qquad Q=\frac{\kappa}{e}\sum_nJ_n . \]
